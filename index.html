FULL BRIEF

In April 2023, fighting broke out between Sudan's military and the Rapid Support Forces paramilitary group. Within months it became the world's fastest-growing displacement crisis, pushing over 11 million people from their homes.

North Darfur is the epicentre. El Fasher, its capital, remains under siege. Aid convoys are blocked. Hospitals have been shelled. The UN has officially declared famine in multiple regions — the first such declaration anywhere in the world since Somalia in 2011.

International news covered the story for about three weeks. Then other headlines came. The people of North Darfur did not get to leave with the cameras.
